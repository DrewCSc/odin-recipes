Miso Ramen

About Miso Ramen

Miso ramen is a Japanese noodle soup that features a rich and savory miso-based broth, combined with various toppings and ingredients. This dish is known for its deep, umami flavors and comforting texture, making it a beloved choice among ramen enthusiasts.


Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles
  • Miso paste
  • Chicken or pork broth
  • Soft-boiled eggs
  • Green onions
  • Bean sprouts
  • Nori (seaweed)
  • Chashu pork

Instructions

  1. Prepare the miso broth by combining miso paste with chicken or pork broth and heating it until well mixed.
  2. Cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions and set aside.
  3. Soft-boil the eggs and slice them in half.
  4. Assemble the ramen bowls by placing the cooked noodles in the bowl, pouring the miso broth over them, and adding the soft-boiled eggs, green onions, bean sprouts, nori, and chashu pork on top.
  5. Serve the Miso ramen hot and enjoy!
