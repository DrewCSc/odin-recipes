Spicy Tonkotsu Ramen


About Spicy Tonkotsu Ramen

Spicy Tonkotsu ramen is a Japanese noodle soup that features a rich and creamy pork bone broth, combined with spicy seasonings and toppings. This dish is known for its bold flavors and satisfying texture, making it a favorite among ramen enthusiasts.

Ingredients

  • Tonkatsu broth
  • Spicy chili oil
  • Soft-boiled eggs
  • Green onions
  • Noodles
  • Chashu pork
  • Bean sprouts
  • Nori (seaweed)
  • Eggs

Instructions

  1. Prepare the tonkatsu broth by simmering pork bones for several hours until rich and creamy.
  2. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions and set aside.
  3. In a separate pan, heat the spicy chili oil and sauté the chashu pork until it is cooked through and slightly caramelized.
  4. Soft-boil the eggs and slice them in half.
  5. Assemble the ramen bowls by placing the cooked noodles in the bowl, pouring the tonkatsu broth over them, and adding the sautéed chashu pork, soft-boiled eggs, green onions, bean sprouts, and nori on top.
  6. Drizzle additional spicy chili oil over the ramen for extra heat, if desired.
  7. Serve the Spicy Tonkotsu ramen hot and enjoy!
