Shoyu Ramen

About Shoyu Ramen

Shoyu ramen is a Japanese noodle soup that features a clear and savory soy-based broth, combined with various toppings and ingredients.This dish is known for its balanced flavors and light yet satisfying texture, making it a popular choice among ramen enthusiasts.

Ingredients

  • Ramen noodles
  • Soy sauce
  • Chicken or pork broth
  • Soft-boiled eggs
  • Green onions
  • Bean sprouts
  • Nori (seaweed)
  • Chashu pork

Instructions

  1. Prepare the soy-based broth by combining soy sauce with chicken or pork broth and heating it until well mixed.
  2. Cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions and set aside.
  3. Soft-boil the eggs and slice them in half.
  4. Assemble the ramen bowls by placing the cooked noodles in the bowl, pouring the soy-based broth over them, and adding the soft-boiled eggs, green onions, bean sprouts, nori, and chashu pork on top.
  5. Serve the Shoyu ramen hot and enjoy!
